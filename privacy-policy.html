Japan Cycle Guide — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Japan Cycle Guide provides educational information about bicycle safety and road rules in Japan. This policy explains how the app and its advertising services may handle information.

Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location to use its main features. The app may check your device's network connection to indicate connectivity. The developer does not use this check to identify you.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising ID, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements. The information handled can depend on your device settings, consent choices, and Google's services and policies. The developer does not receive direct access to all information collected by Google.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services. You can manage advertising and privacy settings in Android settings and, where presented, Google's consent choices.

How the developer uses information

The developer does not operate an account system or developer-run analytics service in this app. The developer does not sell personal information. Google Mobile Ads may process information as described above to deliver and support advertising.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may have their own age-related requirements and data practices. The app's target audience and ad settings should match its actual intended audience and applicable Google Play policies.

Data retention and security

The developer does not maintain a user account database for this app. Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's retention, security, and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on the Japan Cycle Guide Google Play Store listing.

This policy describes the current app design as understood by the developer. If app features, SDK settings, or data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declarations must be updated to match.
